description-long: "Curtis Epperson creates a unique style of contemporary art he calls **Word Mosaic Art**. Every piece is made entirely from carefully hand-drawn words and phrases related to the subject, with no word ever repeated. From a distance, viewers see a striking portrait or image. Up close, they discover hundreds of hidden words that tell the story behind the artwork.

Blending fine art, puzzles, and education, each piece invites people to slow down, explore, and interact with the art in a way few traditional works can. Whether it's a beloved character, animal, landmark, or icon of pop culture, every artwork rewards curiosity with fascinating details hidden in plain sight.

Inspired by a lifelong love of art, puzzles, and learning, Curtis describes his work as **Intellectual Art for the Puzzled Mind.** His goal is to create artwork that not only looks beautiful on a wall but also sparks conversation, encourages discovery, and gives viewers a reason to come back again and again."
