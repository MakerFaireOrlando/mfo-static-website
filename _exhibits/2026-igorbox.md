description-long: "IgorBox is a show control platform for haunted attractions, escape rooms, and themed entertainment, designed and built by a two-person maker team in Port Orange, FL. Our exhibit is a live, hands-on mini haunt: press a button, trip a sensor, and watch animatronics move, lights synchronize to audio, and props fire in perfectly timed sequences.

Behind the scares, we'll show you how it all works. Everything on display is built in-house, from the ESP32-based circuit boards to the embedded firmware to the browser-based show designer that choreographs it all. We'll have bare PCBs, open enclosures, and the actual design tools on screen, and we're happy to go as deep as you want on hardware design, real-time control, DMX lighting, pneumatics, and audio-synchronized effects.

Whether you're a home haunter, an escape room builder, a maker curious about embedded systems, or a kid who wants to make a monster jump, come push the big red button. Our controllers are battle-tested every October at Rotting Orchards, our free community charity haunted attraction, and we love talking shop about the craft of the perfectly timed scare."
