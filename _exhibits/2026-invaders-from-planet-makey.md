description-long: "The Makeys have invaded the farm, and they’re making off with the cows! It’s up to you to stop them with the Color Cannon, an interactive air-powered launcher filled with lights, sounds, and colorful chaos.

Watch the Makey target carefully as it changes colors, then find a soft foam ball that matches. Load it into the cannon, line up your shot, and fire! Hit the Makey with the correct color to score and stop the abduction. Choose the wrong color and your shot won’t count, so you’ll need to think fast and aim carefully.

The experience comes alive with illuminated cannon effects, sound feedback, and environmental lighting that react to every shot. Behind the scenes, a custom ball recovery and retrieval system keeps the action moving so players can jump right back in.

It’s part carnival game, part color-matching challenge, and part interactive engineering experiment—all wrapped up in a frantic mission to save the cows."
