description-long: "Learn to Solder has been part of Maker Faire Orlando for years. You sit down at a station, an instructor shows you what to do, and you solder a light-up pin yourself. This year's pin is Alien Abduction. You put the board together, solder the parts in place, and take the finished pin with you. No experience needed. Kids and adults both do it, and most people who sit down have never held a soldering iron before. In one Faire weekend, more than 900 people learned to solder at these tables, and the Faire has aimed as high as 1,500 people in two days. You leave with a pin that lights up and works, and you made it."
