description-long: "Randall Paints Presents: Crescent Hollow
Discover Crescent Hollow, a fictional New England town where strange traditions, mysterious creatures, and forgotten history hide around every corner. Through original illustrations, maps, field guides, props, signage, and handcrafted world-building, visitors can explore an immersive universe that continues to grow with every new piece of art.

The exhibit also features Lawnskee Ball, an original game inspired by classic lawn games, mini golf, and vintage recreation clubs. Visitors can learn how the game was designed, see prototypes, and even play while helping shape its future through real-world testing and feedback.

Whether you’re drawn to storytelling, illustration, game design, or themed entertainment, Crescent Hollow offers a behind-the-scenes look at creating an original world from the ground up one sketch, prop, and game at a time."
