description-long: "The Fallout Xperience is a traveling immersive cosplay exhibit created and directed by Tyler and Autumn, two fans of Fallout and cosplay who turned their passion for world-building, props, and community into something much bigger than a convention display.

What started as a small collection of handmade props has grown into a full Wasteland themed experience complete with large scale builds, interactive photo areas, and screen inspired details crafted with love, creativity, and a whole lot of super glue.

As co directors, Autumn and Tyler share the vision of using cosplay for more than just entertainment. Every event is an opportunity to give back. Over the years, The Fallout Xperience has raised thousands of dollars for charity, supporting causes like St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, The Trevor Project, and other organizations that make a real difference in people’s lives.

The mission is simple: Do good, build cool things, and bring people together through the Fallout universe.

Whether you visit the booth to take photos, see the props, meet the team, or support the charity efforts, you become part of the Wasteland family that makes this project possible.

Donate $5 dollars per person to charity to enter and get a guided tour from one of our Wasteland survival experts.

$10 per person gets you a tour and some tokens to try and win some swag/prizes on the way out

$15 dollars gets you all that and an entry to try and win one of our daily or weekend grand prize giveaways"
