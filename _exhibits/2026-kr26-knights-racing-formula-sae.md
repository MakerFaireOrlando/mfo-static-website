We combine innovative engineering, testing, and hands-on fabrication to improve vehicle performance continually. Beyond engineering, our members develop essential skills in budgeting, sponsorship relations, and marketing through a comprehensive business presentation.

Driven by teamwork, passion, and commitment to excellence, UCF Formula SAE provides students with real-world experience and prepares them for a successful career.

We proudly represent UCF in national Formula SAE competitions.
