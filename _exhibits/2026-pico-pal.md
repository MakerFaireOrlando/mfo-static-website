description-long: "Pico Pal is an open-source handheld and devkit that pairs a nostalgic late-'90s form factor with modern hardware. Built on the Raspberry Pi RP2350B and an ESP32 co-processor, it has a 2.6-inch IPS display, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, a real-time clock, and a motion sensor.

It plays retro games from files or original cartridges, and can back up a cartridge, play your save, and write it back to the cart.

It's also a full music player. Pico Pal handles lossless and common formats with stereo speakers, a dedicated headphone amp, and Bluetooth audio, plus album and artist browsing with animated backgrounds. Its sunrise alarm clock wakes you gently and plays sleep sounds at night, and it tracks your steps while you listen.

Motion controls are built in throughout. Tilt it like a camera and press A to take a screenshot, twist it to open the menu, and it sleeps on its own in your pocket. Games can use motion too. Screenshots and photos can be set as your home screen.

Files move over USB or Wi-Fi, and it can output video to a TV. For makers, it doubles as a hardware hacking tool, universal remote, and battery-powered RP2350 testbed. The cartridge slot also works as an expansion port over SPI and I2C, designed for future accessories like a battery pack, camera, link port, or extra display.

The schematics, firmware, and bill of materials are all public, so you can repair it, modify it, or build your own software for it.

Pico Pal is your device."
