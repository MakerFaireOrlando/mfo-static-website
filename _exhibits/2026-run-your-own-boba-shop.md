description-long: "Run Your Own Boba Shop is a hands-on food science, STEM, and entrepreneurship experience where participants become the makers and create their own boba drink from beginning to end.
Start with a color-changing cup and watch what happens when ice is added. Measure the ice and liquid using marked portions while exploring temperature, volume, ratios, and consistency.
Next, discover the science of boba by comparing chewy tapioca pearls with bursting popping boba. How can two little spheres look similar but have completely different textures and structures?
Then it’s time to become the boba maker! Choose your boba, assemble your drink, and follow a simple production process to complete your creation.
Finally, turn your creation into your own product by personalizing a “_____’s Boba Shop” label and adding it to your finished cup. Makers can then stop at our self-serve photo station to photograph what they created.
Through one fun and familiar drink, participants explore food science, measurement, math, process design, creativity, branding, and entrepreneurship.
Don’t just drink it—MAKE IT! 🧋"
