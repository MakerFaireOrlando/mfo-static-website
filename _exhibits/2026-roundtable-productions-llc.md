description-long: "Roundtable Productions, LLC returns to Maker Faire Orlando with a hands-on showcase spanning nearly 30 years of themed entertainment fabrication, kinetic art, and original character IP. Founded by Creative Director Stefan Price, Roundtable builds custom props, special effects, and immersive experiences for clients including Disney Parks, Universal, and WWE — and this booth brings that same craft down to Maker Faire scale.

Highlights include original 3D stroboscopic zoetropes that bring sculpted figures to life through spinning illusion, a look at ongoing animatronics R&D from Roundtable's collaborative work with the MakerFX animatronics group, and Bits & Bots — Stefan's retro-futurist robot IP universe exploring technology as both aide and menace — featuring artwork, prints, 3D-printed figures, and hands-on play with Scrap Sweepers, an original tabletop game set in the Bits & Bots world.

Come see props, mechanisms, and stories built by hand, and talk shop with a maker who's spent a career turning fabrication skill into full-blown worlds"
