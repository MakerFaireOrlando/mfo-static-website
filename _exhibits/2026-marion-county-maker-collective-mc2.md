description-long: "The Marion County Maker Collective booth will showcase the creativity, innovation, and collaborative spirit of our community makerspace. Visitors can learn about our makerspace through photos, project displays, membership information, and opportunities to get involved.

Our booth will feature a variety of member-created projects, including a small sumo robot, original works of art, 3D-printed designs, and other examples of the diverse things our members create using technology, traditional craftsmanship, and artistic expression.

In addition to viewing projects, attendees will be invited to participate in hands-on interactive activities that encourage curiosity, creativity, and making. Whether you're an artist, engineer, hobbyist, student, entrepreneur, or simply curious, our booth offers a glimpse into the collaborative environment and wide range of skills, tools, and projects that define the Marion County Maker Collective."
