description-long: "New horizons is a 3lb vertical spinner robot with a focus on speed and aggression. This robot competed at robot ruckus last year and has been completely redesigned over the course of this year. It has competed at a multitude of events in Florida winning Miami robot renegades and having minor success at plenty of other events. 

Pioneer is a 1lb horizontal spinner robot that has been absolutely tearing it up. This robot has podiumed at multiple events and has won 2 over the course of the last year. It's primary weapon makes up for nearly 40% of its total weight. The weapon blade is 5in diameter while the entire robot is only 1.5in tall."
